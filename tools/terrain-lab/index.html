Methods and limits

Orange reproduces current terrain sampling, observer-height heuristic, tile-edge interpolation and half-degree skyline. Purple corrects observer height and ray sampling while retaining the same coarse tile rings. Blue also downloads finer Mapzen tiles. Green uses Mapterhorn with equivalent tile pixel spacing to blue. The application’s PNG decode is reproduced with browser image decoding.

Detailed ring targets: zoom 14 within 2 km, zoom 12 to 10 km, zoom 11 to 60 km, zoom 10 to 150 km for 512-pixel Mapterhorn tiles. Mapzen uses one zoom level higher for its 256-pixel tiles. Rays step at 75% of a pixel, beginning at 20 m, every 0.05° azimuth. Both use Earth curvature and the existing refraction coefficient 0.13. These settings are experimental, not a converged accuracy guarantee. Observer height is ground elevation + 2 m; blank ground elevation uses Mapterhorn at the exact point. Buildings and trees are not separately modelled.

Payload is the sum of encoded tile bytes. Compute time excludes downloading and decoding. Load time includes downloading/cache reads and decoding. Missing rays include any missing distance sample; comparisons exclude incomplete rays. The local server caches tiles on disk; regular browser mode uses the browser HTTP cache. Application localStorage is untouched.
